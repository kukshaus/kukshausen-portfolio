4. Contact form and email

When you press "Send message", your name, email address, optional phone number and message are sent to Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form relay service that forwards the message as an email to my mailbox. According to its privacy policy, Web3Forms does not store submissions after forwarding them. The service runs on infrastructure outside the EU, so your data may be transferred to a third country; this transfer is necessary to handle the request you are making (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR). If you prefer, email or call me directly instead, then no relay service is involved. A hidden anti-spam field in the form drops automated submissions; it collects no data.

I use the data only to answer your request and for any follow-up questions. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for enquiries about a working relationship, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I delete the correspondence once your request is settled, unless statutory retention periods for business correspondence apply.

My mailbox is also hosted by IONOS SE under the same data processing agreement. Data stays within the EU.

Booking a call

The "Book a 20-min call" buttons are plain links to my booking page at Calendly (Calendly LLC, Atlanta, USA). Nothing from Calendly is loaded on this website. If you book a slot, you enter your details on Calendly's site under its privacy notice; I receive the booking to prepare and hold the call. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Calendly is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
